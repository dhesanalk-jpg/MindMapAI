# Market landscape and positioning

This is a directional competitor benchmark based on public product descriptions. Features and pricing change; re-check before presenting externally.

| Product | Publicly described strengths | MindMap AI positioning |
|---|---|---|
| StudyFetch | Upload syllabus/PDFs, build study schedules and connect sessions to study materials. | Add a topic dependency constellation, confidence calibration and plan-rescue workflow. |
| Quizlet | AI practice tests, study guides, PDF summaries and flashcard generation. | Centre the experience on syllabus coverage and adaptive scheduling, not only generated study assets. |
| Knowt | Notes, flashcards, practice tests, learn modes and spaced repetition. | Tie topic-level learning evidence to exam timeline and plan adjustment. |
| MyStudyLife | Timetable, homework, exam tracking, reminders, revision planner, focus timer and AI planner Scout. | Focus on syllabus-topic mastery, source traceability and confidence-versus-performance signals. |
| StudySmarter | Study materials, AI explanations, flashcards, learning paths and spaced repetition. | Differentiate through transparent topic graph, missed-plan recovery and scenario comparison. |
| Vaia | Digital learning materials, summaries, flashcards and study support. | Build a syllabus-first adaptive learning operating system with explainable next steps. |

## Positioning statement

MindMap AI is a syllabus-first adaptive learning planner. It connects document understanding, topic relationships, time planning, practice and progress evidence into one feedback loop.

## Claims to avoid

- "No competitor has this feature."
- "100% accurate syllabus extraction."
- "AI predicts your exam questions."
- "Guaranteed marks improvement."
- "The system knows exactly when you will forget."

Use: "Our proposed differentiator is the combination and workflow, and we will validate it through user testing and competitor research."
